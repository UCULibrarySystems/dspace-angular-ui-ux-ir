Repository license and distribution policy

Policy owner: Uganda Christian University Libraries and Archives.

Author ownership

Authors and other rights holders retain ownership of copyright and other rights in their publications unless a separate written agreement says otherwise. Deposit in VERITAS does not transfer copyright to Uganda Christian University (UCU).

UCU as distributor

By depositing approved material, the depositor authorises UCU to store, preserve, describe, display, index, and distribute that material through VERITAS on the author’s or rights holder’s behalf, subject to the access conditions and license recorded for the item.

UCU acts as a repository distributor and steward. It does not claim ownership of deposited publications, and it will not apply a broader reuse license than the author or rights holder has selected.

Item-level terms control

Users must check each item’s license, rights statement, embargo, and access conditions. Where an item is marked CC BY 4.0, that license governs reuse. Where no open license is shown, access does not by itself grant permission beyond what applicable law allows.

For corrections, changed permissions, or takedown requests, contact the repository team through the feedback form.
