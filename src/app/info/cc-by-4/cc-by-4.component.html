CC BY 4.0 policy for UCU VERITAS

License reference: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.

When CC BY 4.0 applies

CC BY 4.0 applies only to VERITAS items explicitly marked with that license. It permits users to copy, redistribute, remix, transform, and build upon the material for any purpose, including commercially, provided they comply with its terms.

Attribution requirements

Reuse must give appropriate credit to the author or other attribution parties, link to the license, and indicate changes where applicable. Attribution must not imply that an author or UCU endorses the reuse.

UCU’s role

The author or rights holder is the licensor. UCU does not take ownership of the publication; it distributes the material through VERITAS on the author’s behalf and records the selected license. UCU will preserve and make the item available according to the depositor’s authority and repository policy.

Important limits

CC BY 4.0 does not remove rights or obligations that may still apply, including privacy, moral rights, confidentiality, trademark, or third-party rights. The Creative Commons deed is a plain-language summary; users should consult the legal code for the complete terms.
