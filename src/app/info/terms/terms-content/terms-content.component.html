Terms of use

Repository terms of use

These terms describe how Uganda Christian University researchers, students, staff, depositors, and repository users may deposit, access, cite, and reuse material in the Uganda Christian University Digital Institutional Repository.

Policy status: Draft for review and approval by the University Librarian and UCU Legal Counsel. Confirm formal approval before treating these terms as binding institutional terms.

Purpose of the repository

Uganda Christian University supports the preservation and dissemination of research and scholarship as widely as possible. The repository helps make UCU research outputs findable, accessible, interoperable, reusable, and preserved for long-term institutional stewardship.

What may be deposited

Deposited content must be connected to Uganda Christian University. This includes outputs authored by current or former UCU researchers, students, and staff; research conducted at UCU; outputs from UCU-hosted journals, conferences, projects, centres, and collaborations; and works created using UCU facilities or institutional support.

Accepted material may include publications, conference papers, book chapters, monographs, theses, dissertations, research data, presentations, reports, software, images, and other approved scholarly or institutional outputs.

Depositor responsibilities

  • Depositors must have authority or permission to deposit the submitted material.
  • Depositors must ensure that research was produced in accordance with relevant UCU research, ethics, integrity, and funder requirements.
  • Depositors must identify third-party copyright material and obtain permissions where required.
  • Depositors must provide accurate metadata, appropriate files, rights statements, embargo information, and supporting documentation.
  • Human participant data or personal data must be anonymised or shared only under appropriate access controls and consent conditions.

End-user access and reuse

The rights and access statement on the individual item page controls reuse of its files. For an output expressly licensed under CC BY 4.0, users may share and adapt it, including commercially, if they provide appropriate credit, link to the licence, indicate changes, and do not imply endorsement or impose additional restrictions. Read the legal code; the deed is only a summary.

CC BY is not automatically applied to files. Unless a file has a clear licence or rights statement, public access or download does not mean permission for reuse; copyright and neighbouring rights may still apply. Respect third-party material, privacy, ethics, embargoes, and applicable law. Repository descriptive metadata that UCU is authorised to release is made available under CC0 1.0, subject to privacy, confidentiality, third-party rights, and applicable law; CC0 metadata status never licences the described files.

Automated access, scraping and AI use

The repository permits responsible harvesting and indexing of public metadata through published interfaces and standards-compliant search and scholarly discovery services. CC0 metadata may be copied and reused to the extent stated in the repository metadata policy and permitted by law. Use the published OAI-PMH or other API instructions where available; no written permission is needed for ordinary compliant harvesting of public CC0 metadata.

Files and other content may be harvested, mined, copied, or used in automated systems only as permitted by their individual licence, rights statement, access conditions, and applicable law. This agreement does not grant a general licence for AI model training, commercial reuse, or bulk redistribution of files. Where a proposed integration, high-volume harvest, or research text-and-data-mining use is not covered by a published interface or licence, contact the repository team to agree a proportionate method and resolve any required permissions.

The following conduct is prohibited:

  • ignoring published API terms, reasonable rate limits, access controls, embargoes, licences, or rights statements;
  • bypassing or attempting to bypass authentication, CAPTCHAs, technical protection measures, request limits, or other security controls;
  • accessing restricted, embargoed, personal, confidential, or non-public material without authority;
  • using false identities, shared credentials, or automated account creation to obtain access;
  • placing an unreasonable load on the repository or disrupting its availability; and
  • removing or obscuring rights, attribution, provenance, or access information, or using harvested personal data to profile, contact, or make decisions about individuals.

These terms preserve lawful research, accessibility, and interoperability uses and do not prevent uses permitted by an item's licence or applicable copyright exception. They operate alongside applicable Ugandan copyright and data-protection law and other applicable legal obligations. Public visibility does not make restricted files public or override item-level rights.

Monitoring and enforcement

UCU may monitor service health and security, investigate suspected misuse, preserve relevant records subject to privacy and retention requirements, apply proportionate rate limits or access controls, and refer an incident to the appropriate service provider, regulator, law-enforcement authority, rights holder, or UCU Legal Counsel. UCU may pursue remedies available under applicable law after appropriate review; this policy does not predetermine liability or promise that every alleged violation will result in litigation.

Suspected copyright infringement, unlawful automated access, or attempted circumvention should be reported promptly through the repository feedback form or to repository@ucu.ac.ug. Reports should include relevant URLs, dates and times, source IP addresses or user-agent information where available, and supporting evidence.

Repository responsibilities

The repository is responsible for curating, storing, describing, preserving, and sharing approved research outputs according to institutional workflows and applicable funder or policy requirements. The repository may update, restrict, withdraw, or remove material where technical, legal, ethical, administrative, or quality reasons require it.

Where files are removed or access is restricted, metadata may remain visible where appropriate to preserve the scholarly record and support discovery.
