Notice and takedown policy

This policy explains how to request review, restriction, correction, or removal of material in the Uganda Christian University Digital Institutional Repository.

How to request review of an item

Contact the repository team if you are a rights holder, affected person, depositor, or authorised representative and believe that repository material infringes copyright, privacy, confidentiality, ethics, legal obligations, cultural sensitivity, or another legitimate right or policy requirement.

Your request should include:

  • your name and contact details;
  • the full URL, handle, DOI, title, or other information needed to identify the item;
  • the nature of the complaint or request;
  • the reason you believe access should be restricted, corrected, or removed;
  • confirmation that you are the rights holder, affected subject, depositor, or authorised representative where applicable.

Copyright, DMCA and automated-access notices

A rights holder or authorised representative may submit a copyright notice, including a notice intended to meet the requirements of the United States Digital Millennium Copyright Act where that law applies. The repository will assess each notice under the applicable law and UCU policy; describing a notice as a DMCA notice does not by itself determine its legal effect in Uganda.

For a copyright or automated-access complaint, include:

  • identification of the protected work, affected data, or security control;
  • the exact repository URL or URLs and enough detail to locate the material or activity;
  • your full name, organisation, postal address, email address, and telephone number;
  • a good-faith explanation of the alleged infringement, unauthorised collection, or attempted circumvention;
  • evidence available to you, such as timestamps, logs, user-agent strings, screenshots, or rights documentation;
  • a statement confirming your authority to act for the relevant rights holder or affected person; and
  • an electronic signature or equivalent confirmation of the accuracy of the request.

What happens after notification

  • The repository team will acknowledge the request and make an initial assessment.
  • Access to the relevant files may be temporarily disabled while the issue is reviewed.
  • The depositor or responsible unit may be contacted where appropriate and possible.
  • The complainant and depositor may be asked to provide additional evidence or agree a workable resolution.
  • Material may be reinstated unchanged, reinstated with corrections, restricted, superseded, or removed from public access.
  • For suspected unauthorised automated access, UCU may preserve evidence and apply proportionate technical safeguards while the incident is reviewed.

Where a final resolution is not immediately possible, the item files may remain withdrawn from public access while metadata remains available where appropriate.

Counter-notices and review

A depositor or affected party may challenge a restriction or removal by providing evidence of permission, ownership, a valid licence, an applicable exception, or another basis for reinstatement. UCU will consider the available evidence and may seek legal, ethical, or technical advice before deciding whether to restore, amend, restrict, or permanently remove access.

Contact details
