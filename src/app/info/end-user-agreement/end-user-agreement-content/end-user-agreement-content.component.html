1. About this agreement

This agreement governs your use of the Uganda Christian University Digital Institutional Repository (the “Repository”), operated by Uganda Christian University (UCU) through Uganda Christian University Libraries and Archives. The Repository, branded as Veritus, provides discovery and access to scholarly and institutional outputs. By accessing restricted services, registering, or selecting “I have read and I agree”, you confirm that you have read this agreement and will comply with it and with the Repository policies linked below.

This agreement applies alongside, and does not replace, applicable UCU policies, the Repository Terms of Use, the Privacy Policy, item-level licences and access statements, and applicable Ugandan law. If they conflict, mandatory law and the applicable item-level rights and access conditions take precedence.

2. Access and accounts

Public records may be searched and viewed without an account. An account or assigned role may be required for submission, review, workflow, collection or community administration, and other restricted functions. You must use your own authorised account, provide accurate registration details, protect your credentials, and promptly report suspected compromise to repository support. You are responsible for activity carried out using your account, except where applicable law provides otherwise.

Access to a role or collection is granted by UCU and may be limited, reviewed, suspended, or withdrawn where required for security, workflow, policy, or legal reasons. Do not share credentials or attempt to use another person’s account.

3. Permitted use and responsible harvesting

You may use the Repository for research, teaching, learning, scholarship, and other lawful purposes. You may cite, link to, and harvest openly released descriptive metadata through documented interfaces, including OAI-PMH, subject to the Repository’s metadata policy, technical guidance, reasonable rate limits, and applicable licences and law. Public availability of metadata does not authorise harvesting or reuse of restricted personal, confidential, or otherwise protected information.

You must not access or attempt to access systems, accounts, files, or personal data for which you lack permission; bypass authentication, embargoes, access controls, or security measures; disrupt service; introduce malicious code; misrepresent your identity; or use automated requests in a way that unreasonably impairs availability or violates an item’s rights or access conditions. Nothing here prohibits lawful research or compliant use of published repository interfaces.

4. Copyright, licences, and attribution

Research outputs remain subject to the rights of their authors, creators, publishers, funders, and other rights holders. UCU does not claim ownership of every deposited work. A file’s item page, rights statement, and licence determine the permissions granted for that file. Access or download alone does not grant permission to reproduce, adapt, redistribute, or use a work commercially, except where the displayed licence or applicable law permits it.

Where a work is expressly labelled CC BY 4.0, the licence permits sharing and adaptation, including commercial use, provided you give appropriate credit, link to the licence, indicate changes, do not imply endorsement, and do not impose additional restrictions. Read the CC BY 4.0 legal code; the deed is a summary, not the licence itself. No blanket CC BY licence is created by this agreement.

Repository metadata may be made available under CC0 only where UCU has authority to do so and subject to privacy, confidentiality, third-party rights, and applicable law. A metadata licence does not license the files described by that metadata, personal data, UCU names or marks, or rights belonging to another person.

5. Personal data and privacy

UCU processes personal data for repository administration, account and access management, scholarly recordkeeping, security, and related purposes described in the Repository Privacy Policy. UCU and users must handle personal data lawfully and respectfully, including in accordance with Uganda’s Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 and applicable regulations. Do not use the Repository to disclose personal, sensitive, confidential, or identifiable information for a purpose that is not authorised. Use the Privacy Policy for information about data-subject requests and contact routes.

6. Deposited content and notices

Users must respect copyright and neighbouring rights under Uganda’s Copyright and Neighbouring Rights Act, 2006, as amended, as well as applicable research ethics, confidentiality, contractual, and institutional requirements. If you believe an item infringes rights, exposes personal data, breaches an access condition, or is otherwise unlawful, report it through the Repository’s notice-and-takedown or feedback contact. UCU may restrict access while a report is assessed; reports are reviewed fairly and are not an automatic finding of infringement.

7. Security, misuse, and enforcement

UCU may monitor and log technical activity for security, service operation, troubleshooting, and investigation in accordance with the Privacy Policy and applicable retention controls. Suspected unauthorised access, interference, or other cyber-related conduct may be referred for assessment under applicable law, including Uganda’s Computer Misuse Act, 2011, as amended. Any restriction, report, or legal action will be determined by UCU through appropriate procedures; this agreement does not predetermine liability or guarantee a particular enforcement outcome.

8. Availability and changes

UCU aims to maintain a reliable Repository but may perform maintenance, change features, or temporarily restrict access to protect users, records, and services. Repository descriptions are provided to support discovery and should be checked against the original work when accuracy is critical. Nothing in this agreement excludes a right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded.

UCU may revise this agreement through its institutional approval process. The Repository should display the effective date and notify users where a material revision requires renewed acceptance. Continued use alone will not be treated as consent where applicable law requires another form of notice or consent.

9. Governing law and contact

This agreement is intended to be interpreted consistently with the laws of Uganda and applicable UCU policies, subject to mandatory legal protections and any competent jurisdiction. For questions or reports, contact Uganda Christian University Libraries and Archives at repository@ucu.ac.ug.
