UCU Libraries and Archives

Repository curation policy

Uganda Christian University Libraries and Archives curates the Uganda Christian University Digital Institutional Repository to make trusted University knowledge discoverable, accessible, responsible, and sustainable for present and future communities.

Purpose and values

This policy guides the selection, description, review, publication, and continuing stewardship of material in the repository. Curation supports Uganda Christian University's commitment to Christ-centred education, academic excellence, integrity, service, stewardship, respect for persons, and constructive engagement with Uganda, Africa, and the wider world.

The repository seeks to represent UCU scholarship accurately and responsibly while promoting open access where this is lawful, ethical, and appropriate. Curation decisions balance discoverability and reuse with copyright, privacy, confidentiality, cultural sensitivity, research ethics, and institutional obligations.

Scope of curation

UCU Libraries and Archives may curate research and scholarly outputs connected to Uganda Christian University, including journal articles, theses and dissertations, conference papers, books and chapters, reports, teaching and learning resources, research data, software, creative works, and approved institutional or archival records.

Material is considered where it has a clear UCU connection, is within the repository's technical and operational capacity, and can be made available in a way consistent with the repository terms of use.

Curation process

Repository staff review submissions according to their content type and intended access conditions. Review is proportionate to the material and is not a substitute for academic peer review, legal advice, or research ethics approval.

  • confirm the item's UCU affiliation, eligibility, and collection placement;
  • check that descriptive metadata is sufficient for identification, discovery, citation, and context;
  • review author, contributor, funder, rights, licence, and access information where supplied;
  • assess files for readability, integrity, accessibility, format suitability, and consistency with the record;
  • identify embargoes, personal data, confidential information, ethical restrictions, or third-party rights concerns;
  • apply appropriate subject terms and contextual links, including relevant UCU, Uganda, African, and global research contexts where justified;
  • request clarification, corrections, replacement files, or supporting evidence when needed before publication.

Metadata, accessibility, and discoverability

Curation aims to provide clear, accurate, and inclusive descriptions that support access by students, researchers, communities, search engines, and scholarly discovery services. Repository staff may standardise metadata, improve titles and abstracts, add identifiers and relationships, or correct obvious errors while retaining the scholarly meaning of the record.

Depositors should provide accessible files and meaningful descriptions of non-text content whenever possible. Staff may advise on accessible alternatives, but responsibility for the scholarly content and any specialist accessibility remediation remains with the depositor or responsible UCU unit.

Rights, ethics, and sensitive material

Depositors must have authority to deposit their material and must identify relevant licences, permissions, embargoes, and restrictions. UCU Libraries and Archives may limit access, defer publication, remove files pending review, or decline material where copyright, privacy, confidentiality, research ethics, security, cultural heritage, or other legitimate concerns cannot be resolved.

Metadata will remain publicly visible wherever reasonable to preserve the scholarly record, unless disclosure itself would create a legal, ethical, safety, or privacy risk.

Ongoing stewardship

Curation continues after publication. Repository staff may enhance metadata, connect related records, correct errors, manage access changes, retain superseded versions where appropriate, and take preservation actions in line with the preservation policy.

UCU Libraries and Archives may withdraw or restrict material where a valid rights, ethical, legal, quality, or safety concern is raised. Requests are considered through the notice and takedown process.

Roles and responsibilities

  • Depositors provide accurate information, appropriate files, and evidence of relevant rights or approvals.
  • UCU Libraries and Archives manages repository curation, guidance, metadata quality, access review, and routine stewardship within available resources.
  • Academic units and research offices support depositors in meeting disciplinary, funder, ethical, and institutional requirements.
  • Repository users should respect licences, attribution requirements, access conditions, and applicable law when using repository material.

Review and contact

This policy is reviewed periodically by UCU Libraries and Archives to reflect University processes, user needs, legal and ethical obligations, and developing repository practice. For deposit, metadata, access, or curation questions, contact the repository team through the feedback form.
