Uganda Christian University open access policy

Policy owner: Uganda Christian University Libraries and Archives.

Purpose

UCU supports open access to research and scholarly outputs where this is lawful, ethical, and consistent with contractual, publisher, funder, privacy, and confidentiality obligations. VERITAS provides a trusted institutional service for making eligible outputs discoverable and accessible.

Deposit and access

Authors are encouraged to deposit bibliographic records and, where permitted, an accepted manuscript, published version, dataset, or other approved output. Repository staff may apply embargoes, restricted access, or metadata-only records where required.

Rights and licences

Authors retain their rights. UCU distributes deposited content on their behalf under the license or rights statement selected for the item. Authors should confirm publisher and co-author permissions before deposit and choose an open license only where they have authority to do so.

Responsible openness

Open access does not override protection of personal data, confidential information, ethical obligations, cultural interests, third-party copyright, or security-sensitive material. UCU may review, correct, restrict, or withdraw material when necessary.
